Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

This site, the "Rank Rex Uploader" app (YouTube) and the "Content Studio" app (TikTok) are run by E The Experience / Creek Bay Records for his own channels and accounts.

This website

This site doesn't use accounts, forms, cookies or analytics, and it doesn't collect personal information.

Rank Rex Uploader (YouTube)

  • Rank Rex Uploader is a private tool that uploads videos to YouTube channels owned by E The Experience, and reads view counts for those channels' own videos.
  • It is used only by its owner. It does not access, collect, store, sell or share data about anyone else.
  • It uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's Privacy Policy applies.
  • Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings.

Content Studio (TikTok)

  • Content Studio is a private tool that publishes videos made by Creek Bay Records (music visualizers, ranking shorts and 3D character videos) to TikTok accounts owned by E The Experience / Creek Bay Records, on a daily schedule.
  • It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own TikTok accounts. It does not access, collect, store, sell or share data about any other TikTok user.
  • From TikTok it uses only: the basic profile of the owner's own accounts (display name, avatar and open ID, to confirm which account is posting), the posting permission for those accounts, and the post status of its own uploads. It does not read followers, messages, comments or other users' content.
  • It uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also apply.
  • Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions (or Manage app permissions), then remove Content Studio.

What data is stored, and how to delete it

  • Stored: an OAuth refresh token for each of my own YouTube channels and TikTok accounts (kept as encrypted secrets in my private GitHub repository), the view, like and comment counts of my own channels' videos, and the post IDs of videos Content Studio published.
  • The video counts are refreshed every day and overwritten with the latest numbers. No data about other users or other channels is collected.
  • To delete everything: revoke access at Google third-party access settings (YouTube) or in TikTok's app permissions settings (TikTok), which immediately stops all access; the stored tokens and stats are then deleted from the repository within 7 days. You can also request deletion at the email below.

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Content Studio uses TikTok's developer APIs; see the TikTok Privacy Policy. Our own terms are at Terms of Service.
