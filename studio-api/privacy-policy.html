Privacy Policy

Content Studio App · contentstudioapp.vercel.app · Last updated November 14, 2026

Content Studio App ("we", "the app") is a web tool at contentstudioapp.vercel.app that lets creators publish their own videos to their own TikTok accounts. This policy explains what the app uses, what it keeps, and how to remove it.

What we get from TikTok

  • When you connect TikTok, you approve these permissions on TikTok's own page: user.info.basic (your display name, avatar and an account ID), video.upload and video.publish (to send the videos you choose to your account).
  • We also ask TikTok for your current posting options (privacy choices, whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, the maximum video length) each time you post, and for the status of the posts you made with the app.
  • We never read your followers, following list, messages, comments, likes, or anyone else's content.

What we keep

  • Your TikTok login: stored only inside an encrypted session in your own browser (local storage). It is not saved on our servers. Removing the account in the app, or clearing your browser data, deletes it.
  • Your videos: a video you upload goes to temporary storage only so it can be sent to TikTok. It is deleted as soon as the post is sent, and any upload that was never posted is deleted within 24 hours.
  • Your caption and settings: sent to TikTok with your post and not kept by us.
  • We don't use cookies for tracking, analytics or ads, and we don't sell or share your data with anyone.

The site owner's accounts

The app is also used by its owner (Creek Bay Records) to publish its own channels' videos to its own TikTok accounts on a schedule. For those accounts only, the login is stored encrypted in the owner's private repository. No other user's data is ever stored there.

Removing access and deleting data

  • In the app: click Remove next to your account. This deletes your session from your browser.
  • On TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions (or Manage app permissions) → remove Content Studio App. This stops all access immediately.
  • Questions or deletion requests: etheemusic@gmail.com. We reply within 7 days.

TikTok

Content Studio App uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API. It is not made, endorsed or sponsored by TikTok. TikTok's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also apply. Our own terms: Terms of Service.

Children

The app is not for anyone under 13 (or the minimum age to use TikTok where you live).
