Content Studio App Open Content Studio App

Post your videos to TikTok from your browser

Content Studio App is a free web tool for creators. Upload a short video you made, write the caption, choose who can see it and what viewers can do, and publish it straight to your TikTok account, or send it to your TikTok drafts to finish in the app.

Connect TikTok and start

Your videos

Upload an MP4, MOV or WebM up to 64 MB from your computer or phone. Your file is used only to post it and is deleted right after.

Your settings

Pick the audience (everyone, followers, friends or only you), and turn comments, duets and stitches on or off. Nothing is chosen for you.

Your account

Sign in with TikTok. Content Studio App only posts the videos you choose, on the account you connect, and never reads your followers, messages or other content.

How it works

  1. Click Connect TikTok and sign in on TikTok's own page.
  2. Choose a video from your device and preview it.
  3. Write your caption, choose who can view it and which interactions to allow, and add a content disclosure if it promotes a brand.
  4. Click Post to TikTok (or send it to your drafts). You can watch the post status on the same page.
